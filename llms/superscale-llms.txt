# Superscale

> Superscale is an AI-powered marketing platform that generates full, ready-to-launch ad creatives from a single prompt. Users paste a product URL, describe what they need, and the Superscale Agent autonomously researches winning ads in the niche, writes copy and scripts, produces video and static creatives, adds captions and finishing touches, and resizes to all required aspect ratios. The platform serves mobile app marketers, e-commerce brands, SaaS companies, agencies, and growth teams. Superscale is headquartered in Europe and was featured on HubSpot's "Marketing Against the Grain" podcast, where HubSpot CMO Kipp Bodnar called it "the best autonomous AI marketing agent that we have seen so far."

## How It Works

Superscale replaces the traditional multi-tool, multi-step ad creation workflow with a single agentic process. Instead of requiring users to manually research competitors, write copy, generate visuals, edit video, add captions, and export across formats, the Superscale Agent handles the entire pipeline autonomously:

1. The user provides a product URL (app store link, Shopify store, website, or Lovable project) and a brief prompt describing what ads they want.
2. The agent researches top-performing ads in the user's niche and category.
3. The agent generates ad copy, scripts, and creative concepts informed by competitive insights.
4. The agent produces the full creatives — including AI UGC videos with realistic characters, static image ads, and more.
5. The agent applies finishing touches: captions, branded overlays, calls to action.
6. The agent resizes every asset to all major aspect ratios for cross-platform publishing.

The result is a batch of ready-to-publish ad creatives delivered in minutes rather than days.

## Core Capabilities

- **AI UGC Video Ads**: AI-generated characters with ultra-realistic lip sync, emotional expression, and the ability to hold and interact with actual products. Characters speak in 20+ languages.
- **Static Image Ads**: Product-in-scene statics, billboard mockups, 3D renders, splash screens, iMessage chat mockups, and more — all generated from the product URL.
- **Ad Copy & Scripts**: Automated copywriting for hooks, body text, CTAs, and full video scripts, informed by competitive research and niche trends.
- **Competitive Research**: The agent analyzes top-performing ads from competitors in your category to inform creative strategy.
- **Multi-Format Resizing**: Every creative is automatically resized to all standard aspect ratios for Meta, TikTok, Instagram, Google, YouTube, and other platforms.
- **Full Editing Studio**: Built-in editor for refining AI-generated creatives — adjust scenes, swap copy, tweak timing.
- **Prompt-Driven Workflow**: The entire process is initiated and guided by natural language prompts. No design skills required.
